How to install it

  1. Click “Download” next to your software: a OneDrive page opens, with no account or sign-up needed. Click “Download” again there and wait for the download to finish (about 100 MB).
  2. Keep the file wherever you like (the Desktop, for example) and double-click it: there is nothing else to install.
  3. The first time it opens, the software shows the “unique identifier of this computer”. Click “Copy” and send it to us on WhatsApp: you receive your licence code, to paste into the software.
  4. You need a Windows 10 or 11 computer with 8 GB of memory. The internet is only needed for the download: the software works offline.

If Windows shows a warning

Your browser may say the file is “not commonly downloaded”, and Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. This is normal for recent software: choose “Keep”, then “More info” and “Run anyway”. If in doubt, compare the fingerprint below, or write to us before opening the file.

Check that the file really is ours

Each file has a fingerprint, written under its name. To check it: in PowerShell, type Get-FileHash followed by the name of the file. If the fingerprint is different, do not open the file and tell us.
